Do I need a visa?

With a passport from an EU country (except Cyprus), the UK, Canada or the US, you don't need a visa for the wedding. Stays of up to 90 days are visa-free, two months with a Bulgarian passport and four months with a German or Canadian one.

With a Venezuelan passport

You need a visa, and you have to get it before you travel: Tunisia has no visa on arrival and no online visa. Apply at the Tunisian embassy or consulate responsible for the country where you live. There is none in Venezuela itself; the Tunisian embassy in Brasília covers Venezuela. As far as we know, a residence permit or a visa for another country doesn't replace it, so ask the embassy. Start early: a visa can take weeks.

With any other passport

Ask the Tunisian embassy or consulate in your country early, in case you need a visa.

Traditional Tunisian attire

For men, the jebba: a long, sleeveless tunic worn over a shirt, often with an embroidered waistcoat (farmla) and loose trousers (seroual). The red felt cap is the chechia, and the leather slippers are balgha.

For women, a Tunisian jebba or a caftan, both often richly embroidered. The fouta and blouza, a wrapped skirt with an embroidered blouse, is another traditional choice for wedding celebrations.

Where to buy it

In the souks of the Medina of Tunis. Souk Et-Trouk, the old tailors' souk, sells jebbas, waistcoats and trousers. Souk Ech-Chaouachine makes the chechia, and Souk El Blaghgia the balgha. Embroidered dresses and caftans are sold in the shops around them.

The weather

May on the coast near Tunis is warm by day, around 24 to 26 °C, and cooler at night, around 14 to 16 °C. The sea is about 18 to 19 °C. Bring a layer for the evening by the sea.

Tunisia tips

Money

The Tunisian dinar (TND) can't be bought outside Tunisia. Change money or use an ATM when you land, and keep your exchange receipts: you need them to change dinars back, and dinars can't be taken out of the country. Cards work in hotels and larger restaurants; carry some cash for taxis and markets.

Plugs

Sockets are types C and E, 230 V. European plugs fit; UK and North American plugs need an adapter.

Language

Arabic and French are spoken everywhere, and English in hotels and many restaurants.
