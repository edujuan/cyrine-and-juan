Do I need a visa?

With a passport from an EU country (except Cyprus), the UK, Canada or the US, you don't need a visa for a stay of up to three months. With any other passport, ask the Tunisian embassy or consulate in your country early: a visa can take weeks.

Tunisia tips

Money

The Tunisian dinar (TND) can't be bought outside Tunisia. Change money or use an ATM when you land, and keep your exchange receipts: you need them to change dinars back, and dinars can't be taken out of the country. Cards work in hotels and larger restaurants; carry some cash for taxis and markets.

Plugs

Sockets are types C and E, 230 V. European plugs fit; UK and North American plugs need an adapter.

Language

Arabic and French are spoken everywhere, and English in hotels and many restaurants.
